The writing desk

The part where you actually write.

Kertomus is not a chat window with a word counter bolted on. The manuscript is the centre of the room: chapters in reading order, versions you can reopen, and a sprint timer that counts words without locking the door.

  • A real manuscript board. Draft, revise, finish, reorder — with a scene board for the messy middle.
  • Version history that stays out of the way. The last 30 snapshots are kept, including before an AI append and before a restore.
  • Sprints, not streaks. Pick 10, 15, 25 or 50 minutes. Watch the delta. Nothing is locked; no guilt is installed.
  • A three-act flow board. Beats link to chapters so the shape of the book is a board, not a feeling.

Canon, not vibes

It remembers what you wrote.

Worlds, locations, characters, items and lore are real records — tagged, linked and searchable — not a paragraph you keep pasting into the prompt. The agent reads the same bible you do.

  • Five kinds of canon. Worlds, locations, characters, items and lore, with per-category counts and full-text search.
  • Links that do something. Entries cross-reference each other, and the agent can create or update them mid-conversation.
  • Reference files live with the entry. Attach Markdown, text, JSON or CSV; read it later without hunting for the folder.
  • Bulk import, sane boundaries. Bring a whole setting in from Markdown, JSON or CSV, with a preview and duplicate skipping.

Fair questions

Things people ask before opening the lamp.

Is my manuscript uploaded somewhere?

Only what you choose to send to a model provider. Your library — chats, canon, chapters, versions, settings — lives in a local SQLite file. There is no Kertomus account and no telemetry. If you use a hosted model, its provider’s policy applies to the text you send it; if you use Ollama or LM Studio, nothing leaves the machine.

Do I need an API key?

You need a model, not an account with us. Bring an OpenAI-compatible key, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Groq, or point it at a local server. Ollama and LM Studio need no key. One provider card per service; a different model per chat.

Does it really run on Linux?

Yes. x64 and arm64 AppImage and tarball builds, with a matching .zsync for AppImageUpdate. Release artifacts are GPG-signed and accompanied by SHA256SUMS-linux-x64 (or -arm64) plus detached signatures.

What is the seventh shelf?

The seventh shelf is not a feature. It is not in the navigation. It is a short story, and it is exactly where it wants to be. The studio will not be taking questions at this time.

Is the lamp real?

The lamp is load-bearing. Please do not move it. The rest of the app is ordinary software: React, Electron, SQLite, a local-first database and a healthy suspicion of cloud sync.
